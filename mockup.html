With 2 or more rings the code carries a text message: ring k has 12 + 6k stars at radius 100 + 40k, the inner ring says how many rings there are and the error-correction level, and all outer rings form one Reed-Solomon codeword. The current scanner reads one-ring codes only.
Ring radius is fixed at 100; all sizes are relative to it. The scanner expects the defaults (star 19.5, logo 180); other values are for looking only. Every star is a full pentagram: each limb is solid or the lighter level, the centre is always solid; the scanner works out the lighter level for itself. The code stores metadata × 10¹⁰ + payload in 40 bits; stars 9 to 12 are check symbols. A member ID is the 10-digit payload followed by payload mod 97.
